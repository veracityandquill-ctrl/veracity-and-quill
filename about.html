About Veracity & Quill

Where truth finds expression and stories find a home.

Our Story

Veracity & Quill was created by Adefunke. T. Agoi and Amina. M. Tarfa from a belief that every voice carries a story worth hearing. We believe words can transform people, communities, and the world. Whether whispered in a poem, shouted on a stage or written into lasting projects.

Our Mission

Our mission is to nurture voices, amplify stories and create a bridge between creativity and impact. To give a voice to the voice less and opressed

Our Vision

To create a world where every voice matters, and where art is not just entertainment, but a tool for the truth, healing and transformation .

• Authenticity
• Excellence
