Meet Our Poets

The heart of Veracity & Quill is found in the voices of the writers we represent. Each poet brings a unique perspective, weaving words into stories that inspire, challenge, and connect.

Chisom Nwankwo

Founding Poet

Chisom became the very first poet to join Veracity & Quill, helping lay the foundation for the agency's journey. Her work reflects sincerity, emotion, and a deep appreciation for storytelling.

Tahira

Poet

Tahira's poetry explores reflection, humanity, and the quiet moments that often go unnoticed. Through thoughtful language, she adds another distinctive voice to the Veracity & Quill family. She is also Amina. M. Tarfa, a co founder of VQ (Veracity and Quill)

Benedict Sule

Poet

Benedict is the newest poet to join Veracity & Quill. His arrival marks another step in the agency's commitment to discovering and supporting emerging literary voices.

Leadership Team

Amina M. Tarfa

Amina leads the podcast division of Veracity & Quill, guiding conversations that celebrate storytelling, creativity, and the diverse voices within our community and beyond.
